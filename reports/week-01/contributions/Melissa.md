Overview
Statistical Summaries are single numbers and methods that give a representation of a large data set. This is a form of descriptive statistical analysis. These numbers describe the central tendency, spread and overall shape of the data. They work as a mathematical engine between raw data loading and the final graphical output.
Statistical summaries include;
•	Mean
•	Median
•	Mode
•	Range
•	Standard deviation
•	Quartiles/ Percentiles
•	Interquartile Range
•	Count: Total number of valid points
•	KDE: Kernel Density Estimation
•	Histogram binning
